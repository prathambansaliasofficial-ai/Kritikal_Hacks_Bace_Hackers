# Kritical Hacks 2026: All You Need to Know

## Overview

Kritical Hacks 2026 is a **24-hour hackathon** bringing together students and professionals to create innovative, practical, and technology-driven solutions.

The hackathon follows an **Open Innovation** format, giving participants the freedom to identify a problem and build a solution within their chosen track.

**Participants must use TinyFish in their projects.** During the pitch, you must explain how you incorporated TinyFish.

Participants can compete in either the **AI/ML Track** or the **Web3 Track**, with separate categories for students and professionals.

## Tracks

### AI/ML Track: Open Innovation (Must use TinyFish)

An open-innovation track where participants can build solutions using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to address a problem or use case of their choice.

- **Student Track:** Undergraduate and postgraduate students from colleges and universities.
- **Professional Track:** Working professionals and independent builders.

**Awards at the end of the hackathon:**

- 1 Winner from Student Track
- 1 Winner from Professional Track
- 1 Runner Up from Student Track
- 1 Runner Up from Professional Track

All AI/ML projects must include a meaningful and functional TinyFish integration.

### Web3 Track: Open Innovation (Must use TinyFish)

An open-innovation track where participants can build solutions using Web3 and blockchain technologies to address a problem or use case of their choice.

The track is open to:

- **Student Track:** Undergraduate and postgraduate students from colleges and universities.
- **Professional Track:** Working professionals and independent builders.

**Awards at the end of the hackathon:**

- 1 Student Winner
- 1 Professional Winner

All Web3 projects must include a meaningful and functional TinyFish integration.

## Mandatory TinyFish Integration

TinyFish integration is **mandatory for every project across both tracks.**

TinyFish provides infrastructure that enables AI applications and agents to interact with the live web. Depending on project requirements, teams may use TinyFish capabilities such as:

| Capability | What it does |
|------------|--------------|
| **Search API** | Retrieve fresh and structured information from the live web. |
| **Fetch API** | Extract clean content from web pages in formats suitable for AI applications. |
| **Web Agent** | Perform goal-driven, multi-step actions on websites using natural-language instructions. |
| **Browser** | Control a cloud browser using frameworks such as Playwright or CDP. |

Teams are free to decide how TinyFish fits into their architecture and use case. However, the integration must be **meaningful, functional, and relevant** to the project.

> **Important:** Simply mentioning TinyFish in the PPT, documentation, or architecture without actual implementation will not be considered sufficient. Teams will be required to **explain and demonstrate** their TinyFish implementation during evaluation.

## Guidelines

- The hackathon is open to participants from all academic backgrounds, branches, specializations, courses, and years, subject to the eligibility criteria of the selected category.
- Participants must register under either the **Student** or **Professional** category within their chosen track.
- Participants can compete in either the AI/ML Track or the Web3 Track.
- Inter-college teams are allowed.
- Inter-specialization and interdisciplinary teams are allowed.
- AI/ML Track projects should meaningfully utilize AI/ML technologies.
- Web3 Track projects should meaningfully utilize Web3/blockchain technologies.
- TinyFish integration is mandatory for all projects across both tracks.
- Teams must participate in all mandatory mentorship, evaluation, and presentation checkpoints.
- Only shortlisted teams will be permitted to continue into the overnight build phase.
- Teams must submit their project and required documentation within the deadlines specified by the organizers.

## Eligibility

Kritical Hacks 2026 is open to students and professionals across disciplines, subject to the requirements of the selected category.

### Student Category

- Undergraduate students
- Postgraduate students
- Students from any academic discipline or year
- Students from any college or university
- Interdisciplinary and inter-college teams are allowed.

### Professional Category
